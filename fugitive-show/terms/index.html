The game has a chat. During a round players can send only emojis and a fixed set of taunts. In the studio lobby of a room they can also type short messages, 80 characters at most. Before your first typed message the game shows these rules and asks you to accept them.

  • Be kind. No insults, harassment, threats, hate speech, sexual content or spam.
  • Do not share personal information about yourself or anyone else: real names, addresses, phone numbers, social media accounts.
  • Do not use the chat or your player name to advertise, scam, or ask other players for money, accounts or passwords.
  • Swear words and slurs are hidden automatically in messages, player names and room messages, in the game's six languages. The filter is not perfect: a message that gets past it still breaks these rules.

  • Tap or click a message to report its sender. The report opens an email to us with the player's name, their player ID, the room code and their last messages. We read every report and use reports to improve the filter and the game's moderation.
  • You can turn the chat off at any time in Settings.

Messages are written by players, not by us, and they are not reviewed before they are sent. If someone threatens you or anyone else, leave the room and report them; if you are in danger, contact the local authorities.
