Privacy Policy

The Fugitive Show · Overdose Studio Buenos Aires · Last updated 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information The Fugitive Show (the "game") uses and why. The game is made by Overdose Studio Buenos Aires, Argentina. Contact: overdosestudiobuenosaires@gmail.com.

No account, no ads, no tracking

You do not create an account to play. The game shows no advertising, does not sell or share your data for advertising, and does not use advertising IDs. We do not collect your name, email, contacts, location, photos or any other personal file.

What stays on your device

Your settings, the name you type for your character, your in-game money, fame and unlocked items are saved only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Online play

Online rooms run on Unity Gaming Services (Unity Technologies). When you host or join a room the game:

  • signs in anonymously with Unity Authentication, which gives your installation a random player ID;
  • sends game traffic through Unity Relay servers, which handle your IP address to connect players;
  • shares the name you typed and your in-game results with the other players in the same room.

This information is used only to make online play work. Unity processes it under the Unity Privacy Policy. Solo play against bots needs no connection.

Technical data

The Unity engine may send basic technical information about your device (model, operating system, performance) to Unity to keep the engine working, as described in Unity's privacy policy. It does not identify you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your choices

You can play offline against bots at any time. To ask Unity to delete the anonymous player ID linked to your online play, or for any privacy question, write to us at the address above and we will help.

Changes

If the game starts using information in a new way, for example cloud saves or leaderboards, we will update this page before that version is released.
