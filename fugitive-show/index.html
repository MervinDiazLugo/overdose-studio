How to play

1

Walk into the studio

Explore the set, pick your character and try the weapons while the host warms up the crowd.

2

The live draw

One or two players become Runners, with 10,000 to gear up. Everyone else is a Hunter, with 3,000 and a bounty to collect.

3

Survive 2:30

Runners get one life. Hunters respawn. Survive and you cash in double.

Everyone is the star once

Roles rotate every round, so everyone gets a turn as the fugitive. The biggest bankroll wins.

A ridiculous cast

Ex-con, biker, wrestler, bride, butcher, cowboy and more, each with their own glasses.

Absurd accessories

Duck, traffic cone, afro, monocle and 20 more. Cosmetic only: no pay-to-win.

A windswept wasteland

Dust, plastic bags and tumbleweeds blow across the map with every gust. The cyberpunk city comes later.
