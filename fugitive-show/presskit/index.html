Description

It's a manhunt, broadcast live. The Fugitive Show is a top-down 2D multiplayer action game set inside a deadly TV show. Each round, one or two players are the Runners and everyone else is a Hunter. Runners must survive 2:30 with whatever they bought; Hunters keep respawning to take them down. Everyone gets at least one turn as the Runner, and the biggest bankroll wins.

Players gather in a TV studio they can walk around, pick a character and plan their purchases without knowing which role they will get. Then comes the live draw: Runners start with 10,000 for weapons, vests and bubbles; Hunters start with 3,000. If the Hunters catch a Runner, the Runner's money is split among them. A Runner who survives cashes in twice what they did not spend.

History

The Fugitive Show started from a simple question: what if the manhunt from The Running Man were a party game to play with friends? Mervin, a Venezuelan indie game developer based in Buenos Aires, began the project in November 2026 with one clear rule: every friend in the group gets a turn as the star of the show.

Features

  • 2:30 rounds. Short, intense matches that are easy to replay.
  • 1 or 2 Runners against everyone. Roles rotate, so everyone is the star at some point.
  • Round economy. Shopping before the draw, bounties for kills and a running bankroll as the score.
  • Hunters respawn; Runners get one life.
  • A lobby you can walk around. A TV studio with a host, cameras and a practice range.
  • Hand-drawn art with a 90s arcade attitude, a cartoon cast with different glasses for each character and 24 ridiculous accessories.
  • 2 to 10 players, private rooms with a code and cross-play between PC and Android.
  • No pay-to-win. Real money buys cosmetics only.
